About

I currently work under the Kernels and Ops team at Tenstorrent. I'm big into novel hardware, open-source, and local systems.

At Stanford, I did gamma-ray detector research with KIPAC at SLAC on GammaTPC... lots of particle physics, nuclear stuff, simulation, ML, and hardware. I studied Physics (Quantum Information) as an undergrad and did quantum computing work with Fraunhofer Gesellschaft SCAI in Germany. After this I discovered how much I enjoy computer systems and graphics, so I did a master's in CS. I ended up spending a lot of time on the LX classes, which was a blast to say the least... got to do stuff all over, from FPGAs to apps.

Free time is often about music. I play piano, cello, and guitar—tried to double major but ended up with a 56-unit minor instead. Used to teach instruments and did piano technician work at a music store back home. I compose and occasionally conduct, and I'm currently working on video game development (including soundtracks) with Seamus Allen.

I also run a homelab (dormlab?) and definitely cause no annoyance for Stanford IT. When my eyes need a break, I read outside, go biking, play board games, or just yap. Twilight Imperium 4e before bed is peak.
